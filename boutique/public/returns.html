POLICIES

Returns & refunds

Last updated: October 2026. This policy must be reviewed by a qualified professional before the store accepts real payments.

Return window

You can request a return within 30 days of delivery.

How to request a return

Contact us with your order number and the reason for the return. We will reply with the return address and instructions. Please do not send items back without contacting us first.

Condition

Items must be returned unused, clean and in their original packaging with all accessories.

Damaged or defective items

If your item arrives damaged, defective or not as described, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will offer a replacement or a full refund, including return shipping.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment card through our payment provider once the return is received and inspected, or as soon as we confirm a damaged/defective item. Depending on your bank, the refund can take 5–10 business days to appear.

Return shipping costs

For returns that are not due to our error, return shipping is paid by the customer. For damaged, defective or incorrect items, we cover it.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order free of charge until it has been passed to our fulfillment partner. Contact us as soon as possible.
