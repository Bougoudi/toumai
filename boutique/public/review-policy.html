POLICIES

Review rules & privacy

Last updated: October 2026. These rules describe how this store actually handles reviews. They are not legal advice and should be reviewed by a qualified professional.

What we publish

Every review is read by a person at HavenLume before it appears. We publish positive, mixed and negative reviews alike: a low rating or a critical opinion is never a reason to refuse a review. Reviews are shown newest first. We don't edit the text or the rating of a review, and we don't write, buy or invent reviews.

What we don't publish

We refuse or hide a review, using the same rules for every rating, only when it:

  • is spam, advertising or contains links;
  • contains insults, hate, harassment or obscene content;
  • reveals personal information (addresses, phone numbers, emails, order numbers — yours or someone else's);
  • is not about the product or the purchase experience;
  • is written by our staff, a competitor or someone paid or rewarded for it;
  • duplicates a review already received.

What “Verified Purchase” means

A review shows the Verified Purchase badge only when it was written through a single-use link sent to the email address of a real HavenLume order for this product that was paid, shipped or delivered, and not cancelled or refunded. Answering “Yes” to “Did you purchase this product from HavenLume?” is not proof of purchase and never adds the badge. Reviews without the badge may still come from real customers.

Incentives

We don't offer discounts, gifts or other rewards for reviews, and we never ask for a positive review. When we email a customer after delivery, we ask for an honest opinion, positive or negative.

What we collect

With a review we store: your rating, public name, title and text, your answers to the two questions, your agreement to these rules, the date, and — only for verified reviews — an internal link to the order. Your public name, rating, title, text and date are published. We don't publish or display your email address, postal address or order number, and we don't store your IP address with your review. Verification links are stored only as a one-way fingerprint and expire after 7 days (30 days for links sent after delivery).

Correcting or removing your review

Contact us from the email address you used (for a verified review) or with the public name, date and text of the review. We can remove a review and erase its content. Because we don't edit reviews, a correction means removing the old review so you can submit a new one. We answer reasonable requests as quickly as we can. See also our privacy policy.
