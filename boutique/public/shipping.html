POLICIES

Shipping policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy must be reviewed by a qualified professional before the store accepts real payments.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the United States, including APO/FPO addresses only where our carrier supports them.

Processing

Orders are processed after your payment has been confirmed by our payment provider. Your order is then passed to our fulfillment partner, who ships it directly to you.

Delivery times

Delivery times depend on the carrier and your location. You will receive an email with your carrier and tracking link as soon as your order ships. We will not promise a delivery date we cannot support.

Shipping costs

Shipping costs, if any, are shown in your cart and at checkout before you pay.

Delays and lost parcels

If your parcel is delayed or tracking stops updating, contact us with your order number and we will investigate with the carrier. If a parcel is confirmed lost, we will send a replacement or issue a refund.

Address errors

Please double-check your address at checkout. Contact us immediately if you notice a mistake; we can only correct it before the order ships.
