    USE & SAFETY

    How to use it safely

    Always

    • Read and follow the manufacturer's instructions supplied with the product.
    • Use only a compatible USB power source.
    • Switch off and disconnect the USB cable when not in use and before cleaning.
    • Check the throw and cable before each use; stop using it if anything looks damaged.

    Never

    • Never use it while sleeping.
    • Never use it on infants, young children, or anyone who can't remove it or switch it off on their own, or who can't feel heat normally.
    • Never use it wet, or wash it with the power source or cable connected.
    • Don't fold or bunch it while heating, and don't place heavy objects on it.

    Manufacturer's safety instructions

      HavenLume makes no medical claims. This throw is a comfort product — it isn't intended to treat or relieve any condition. If you have a medical condition or reduced sensitivity to heat, ask your doctor before use.
