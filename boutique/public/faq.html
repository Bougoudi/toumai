GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Orders & payment

How do I pay?

By card on the secure hosted payment page of iyzico, our payment provider. Your card details are entered on iyzico's page; HavenLume never sees or stores your card number.

When is my card charged?

When you complete payment on the iyzico page. Your order is confirmed only after we receive confirmation of the payment from iyzico.

Do you charge sales tax?

Prices shown are the prices you pay at checkout. If sales tax ever applies to your order, it will be shown clearly before you pay.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. Once an order has been passed to our fulfillment partner we may not be able to change it, but you can still use our returns policy.

Shipping

Where do you ship?

To addresses in the United States.

How long does delivery take?

See our shipping policy. You will receive tracking information by email as soon as your order ships.

How do I track my order?

Use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email, or contact us with your order number.

The product

How is the throw powered?

It connects to a USB power source. The compatible power source and what is included in the box are described in the product details and the instructions supplied with the product.

How do I clean it?

Follow the care label and instructions supplied with the product, and always disconnect the power source first.

Is it safe for children or while sleeping?

Do not use the throw while sleeping, on infants or young children, or on anyone who cannot remove it on their own. Always follow the safety instructions supplied with the product.

Returns

Can I return my order?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery under the conditions in our returns policy.
