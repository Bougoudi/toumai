Orders & payment

How do I pay?

Payment options are shown at checkout.

When is my card charged?

Only when you complete payment on the payment page. Your order is confirmed after we receive confirmation of the payment from our payment provider.

Do you charge sales tax?

Prices shown are the prices you pay at checkout. If sales tax ever applies to your order, it will be shown clearly before you pay.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. Once an order has been passed to our fulfillment partner we may not be able to change it, but you can still use our returns policy.

Where do you ship?

To addresses in the United States. See our shipping policy.

Is it safe for children or while sleeping?

No. Do not use the throw while sleeping, on infants or young children, or on anyone who cannot remove it or switch it off on their own. Always follow the safety instructions supplied with the product.
