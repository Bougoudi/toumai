POLICIES

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy must be reviewed by a qualified professional before the store accepts real payments.

What we collect

When you order: your name, email address, shipping address and, if you provide it, your phone number, plus the products ordered and the order amounts. When you contact us: your name, email and message. Our server also records your IP address with your order for fraud prevention.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your card number. Card payments are entered on the hosted payment page of our payment provider, iyzico, which processes them under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

To process and deliver your order, provide customer support, send you order and shipping emails, prevent fraud, and meet our legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • iyzico (payment processing): your name, email, address and the order amount.
  • Our fulfillment partner and carrier: your name and shipping address, and your phone number only if the carrier needs it for delivery.
  • Hosting and email providers that run our website and send our emails.

Visit statistics (analytics)

We measure how the store is used with our own first-party analytics, hosted on our server. It does not use cookies and does not involve Google Analytics or any advertising network.

  • What is recorded: the page viewed (without its query string), the referring website's domain, shopping steps (product viewed, added to cart, checkout opened, continue to payment) with the product, color, quantity and value, and an estimated country.
  • Country: estimated from your IP address with the DB-IP Lite database (IP Geolocation by DB-IP). The estimate can be wrong, for example with a VPN, proxy or mobile network. We never collect your precise location.
  • No IP address is stored. To count unique visitors, we compute a one-way hash of your IP address and browser with a random value that changes every day and is then deleted, so visits can't be linked across days or to you.
  • Never included: your name, email, phone, address, order number or payment details.
  • Purchases are counted from confirmed payments on our server, not from your browser.
  • Retention: statistics are deleted after about 13 months.

Your choice: use the “Privacy choices” link at the bottom of every page to turn analytics on or off for this browser. We also honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals: when your browser sends one, nothing is measured.

Cookies and storage

The store does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your cart and your privacy choice are kept in your browser's local storage on your device.

Retention

We keep order records as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal purposes, and contact messages as long as needed to answer them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, subject to legal record-keeping obligations. California residents have the rights described in the CCPA/CPRA. Contact us to make a request.

Privacy questions: contact us.
